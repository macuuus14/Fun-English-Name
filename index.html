Definisi & Fungsi

Announcement adalah pernyataan publik resmi tentang suatu fakta, kejadian, atau niat/rencana untuk memberikan informasi kepada orang banyak.

🏗️ Generic Structure

  • Title/Opening: Memberitahu tujuan pengumuman.
  • Content: Detail (What, When, Where, Who).
  • Closing: Narahubung (CP) atau pengirim.

🗣️ Language Features

Simple Present Tense

Digunakan untuk fakta saat ini.

"The school is closed today."

Simple Future Tense

Digunakan untuk rencana acara.

"We will hold a festival."

Exact Nouns

Keterangan waktu & tempat yang jelas.

"Monday, Aug 15th at Hall."

Announcement Text

ATTENTION ALL STUDENTS!

To celebrate our school's 50th Anniversary, the Student Council will hold a "School Festival". There will be many competitions such as singing, dancing, and English speech.

WHEN:

Mon - Wed,
Aug 15th - 17th, 2026

WHERE:

School Field &
Main Hall

All classes must register at least one team for each competition. Registration is free and the deadline is August 10th, 2026.

For more information, please contact: Sarah (Class 9A).

The Student Council

Reading Comprehension

1. What is the purpose of the text?

2. How long is the festival?

3. Who is the contact person?
